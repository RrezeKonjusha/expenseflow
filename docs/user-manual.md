# User manual

## Getting started
1. Open the site and choose **Create account**. Fill in your name, work email and a password of at least 10 characters.
2. Open the activation email and click the link. Sign in.
3. Ask an admin to add you to a department and to the projects you work on.

## Employees
**Create an expense.** Expenses > New expense. Choose the type; the form shows the fields that type needs.
Policy limits: travel 0.40 EUR per km, meals 25 EUR per attendee, equipment up to 1000 EUR, at most 90 days old.
Save creates a draft.
**Submit.** Open the draft and click Submit. You can no longer edit it.
**Rejected?** Read the reason, click Reopen, fix it, and submit again.
**Import.** Expenses > Import. Download the CSV template, fill it in, upload. Valid rows become drafts; the table lists problems per row.
**Find expenses.** Use search (words in the description), status, type and dates.

## Managers
**Approve or reject.** Approvals lists submitted expenses from your department, oldest first.
Approve, or Reject with a reason the employee will see. If the project has no budget left, approval is refused.

## Admins
**People and structure.** Administration > Users, Departments, Projects. Deactivate users instead of deleting them.
Set project members so employees can claim against the project.
**Reimbursement.** Reports > Reimbursement run: pick a date and click Reimburse.
**Reports.** Choose dates, grouping and filters, Run, then Save. Open a saved report to export CSV, Excel or JSON.
**Audit log.** Administration > Audit log shows who did what and when; click a row for details.

## Troubleshooting
| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Too many attempts | Wait one minute; after 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes |
| You are not a member of this project | Ask an admin to add you to the project |
| Project budget would be exceeded | The project has no budget left; contact the admin |
