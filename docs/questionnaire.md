# Stakeholder questionnaire (Google Forms)

Send to 10-20 people who work in companies (relatives, friends, internship colleagues). Summarise answers in the SRS.

1. What is your role? (employee / team lead / finance / other)
2. How often do you pay for work expenses yourself? (never / monthly / weekly)
3. How do you claim them today? (email / Excel / paper / tool: ___)
4. How long until you are reimbursed? (days)
5. What is the most frustrating part of the process? (open)
6. Have you lost a claim or forgotten to submit one? (yes / no)
7. As an approver, what do you need to decide quickly? (open)
8. Which reports would finance need? (by project / department / month / type / other)
9. Would you import expenses from a card statement (CSV)? (yes / no)
10. Which device do you use for this? (desktop / phone / both)
